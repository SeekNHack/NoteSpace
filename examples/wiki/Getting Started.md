# Getting started

Write ordinary Markdown. Connect your thoughts with `[[wiki links]]` and [Markdown links](Home.md).

## Your first page

Run **NoteSpace: New Page**, choose a template and give it a name. Type `[[` for suggestions, then use Go to Definition to follow a link.

## Find your way

Use the pages sidebar, backlinks, full-text search or the link graph. Search `#project` to find pages with that tag.

## Share your work

Export the wiki to a new directory of static HTML pages. Your readers need only a browser.
